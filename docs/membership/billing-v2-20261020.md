# Billing v2 · 2026-10-20

Desktop supports Chinese/CNY Alipay and English/USD PayPal via Paddle or USDT (Solana, BSC, TRON). The authenticated server owns product pricing, membership eligibility, immutable order snapshots and payment confirmation. Server channel readiness controls purchasing; missing credentials never produce a simulated checkout.

| Plan | Monthly USD / CNY | Annual USD / CNY | Credits each month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | 20 / 134 | 120 / 804 | 2,000 |
| Plus | 40 / 268 | 240 / 1,608 | 5,000 |
| Max | 80 / 536 | 480 / 3,216 | 10,000 |

Annual purchases issue twelve monthly grants. All plans are one-time purchases without auto renewal. A renewal starts after the existing plan ends; the checkout shows its activation date. Credit packs are available immediately and expire six calendar months after payment. Pack USD/CNY prices: 29.9/200, 49.9/334, 69.9/468, 99.9/669; base credits 1,500/3,000/5,000/8,000, with an active-member 50% bonus fixed in the order quote. Packs do not extend membership.

New signup and free check-in grants are 10 credits each, valid 24 hours after claim. Existing permanent purchases and historical gifts remain permanent. New credits are allocated by earliest expiry, including image reservations. Unused reservations preserve their original expiry and cannot revive after expiration. A one-minute worker and transaction-time reconciliation deliver due grants and remove expired unused credits.

The ledger displays timestamp, actual tool/model when recorded, supported optional settings, signed credit movement, balance after, expiry and related receipt/order. Existing records without metadata are not backfilled with invented details. Search, type/date filters, paging and current-page CSV are included.

Payment callbacks verify signatures and bound order/provider reference/currency/amount. Completion is atomic and idempotent. A confirmed full refund withdraws unused purchased credits, cancels future grants and removes the refunded membership; partial monetary refunds are recorded for reconciliation. Long-term memory and Brain are free. No unverified speed, queue, concurrency, task-count or support benefits are promised.
